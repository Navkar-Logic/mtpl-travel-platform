## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the comprehensive content mapping and review for the **Terms & Conditions** page implemented on the Mishrikotkar Travels Pvt. Ltd. (MTPL) Phase 1 website. 

The content has been crafted strictly by synthesizing the three authoritative source documents supplied by MTPL:
1. **`Holidays T&C.docx`**: *Holiday Tour Package Terms, Client Declaration & Limitation of Liability* (15,835 characters)
2. **`MTPL_General_Booking_Policy_T&C.docx`**: *General Booking Policy & Terms of Service* (16,220 characters)
3. **`MTPL_Disclaimer.docx`**: *Website Disclaimer* (9,742 characters)

All source files were located in `F:\Navkar Logic Client Data\MTPL\` and thoroughly parsed.

## 2. Phase 1 Website Scope vs. Booking Policy Scope

A fundamental principle established in this review is the clear operational distinction between **Website Usage** and **Travel Booking Contracts**:

| Feature / Dimension | Phase 1 Website Implementation | Treatment in Terms & Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **User Accounts / Customer Portal** | **Not implemented**. No logins, profiles, or account credentials. | Excluded. No terms regarding account passwords or user dashboards. |
| **Online Payment Gateway** | **Not implemented**. No online credit card / UPI checkout. | Excluded. Payments are handled via direct bank transfer / invoice after consultation. |
| **Instant Booking Confirmation** | **Not implemented**. Packages and flights are not instantly booked online. | Clearly stated that website forms are requests for quotation, not confirmed bookings. |
| **Online Visa Application** | **Not implemented**. No online biometric/document processing. | Clarified as professional advisory and documentation facilitation only. |
| **Automated Refund Processing** | **Not implemented**. No automated self-service refund portal. | Clarified that refunds are subject to supplier rules and manual settlement. |
| **Package Itinerary PDFs** | **Implemented**. Dynamic PDF downloads available on package pages. | Governed as copyrighted informational materials for personal travel planning. |
| **Enquiry & Contact Forms** | **Implemented**. Captures traveller name, phone, email, and preferences. | Governed as communication requests. Passport numbers are explicitly not collected via web forms. |
| **WhatsApp Direct Inquiries** | **Implemented**. Pre-filled WhatsApp routing for instant concierge. | Clarified that WhatsApp chats constitute preliminary inquiry, not an inventory block. |

## 4. Analysis of Overlaps, Nuances, and Identified Variations

During cross-examination of the three documents, several key nuances were identified and addressed:

### 1. General Website Terms vs. Holiday Package Terms
* **Finding**: `Holidays T&C.docx` contains an explicit liability cap: *"MTPL's aggregate liability shall not exceed the service/consultancy charges actually paid to MTPL for the affected booking, excluding amounts collected and passed to third-party suppliers"* (Clause 12).
* **Treatment**: This specific monetary liability cap relates to contractual package bookings and has been accurately represented as a term of the specific Holiday Package booking contract, rather than as an absolute blanket exclusion for browsing the website. The general Terms & Conditions preserves mandatory rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

### 2. Dispute Resolution & Arbitration
* **Finding**: Both `MTPL_General_Booking_Policy_T&C.docx` (Clause 21) and `Holidays T&C.docx` (Clause 14) provide for sole arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 in Nagpur. `MTPL_Disclaimer.docx` references jurisdiction of competent courts in Nagpur without mentioning arbitration.
* **Treatment**: The Terms & Conditions page notes that disputes arising from website browsing are subject to the courts at Nagpur, Maharashtra, while contractual travel agreements may incorporate the mutual arbitration mechanism specified in the service booking contract, preserving mandatory statutory consumer forum jurisdictions.

### 3. Tiger & Wildlife Sightings
* **Finding**: Both `MTPL_Disclaimer.docx` (Clause 6) and `MTPL_General_Booking_Policy_T&C.docx` (Clause 9) explicitly state that wildlife sightings (including tigers) can never be guaranteed, and permits are controlled by Forest Departments.
* **Treatment**: Explicitly highlighted under Section H (Jungle Safari & Wildlife Bookings) to protect client expectations regarding Tadoba, Pench, Kanha, and Bandhavgarh tours.

### 4. Electronic Communications and "OK" Approvals
* **Finding**: Both booking documents provide that electronic approval via WhatsApp or Email (e.g. "OK", "CONFIRM", "GO AHEAD") constitutes binding approval for booking commitments.
* **Treatment**: Included in Section D (Enquiries, Quotations & Confirmation) so customers understand the significance of confirming itineraries over WhatsApp or email with MTPL consultants.
